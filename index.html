FAQs

MBA Admission FAQs

Which MBA specialisations does SGT University offer?

The 2-year MBA offers specialisations in Healthcare Management, Fintech, Business Analytics, Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, International Business and Operations, with a dual specialisation option. A 4-year Integrated MBA is also available after 10+2.

What is the eligibility for the MBA at SGT University?

You need a graduation degree in any discipline from a recognised university, with minimum marks as per university norms. Your counsellor will confirm the exact criteria for 2027-28.

Which entrance exams are accepted for MBA admission?

Scores from entrance exams such as CAT or MAT are considered, or you can take the SGT entrance test. Shortlisted candidates then attend a personal interview. Your counsellor will confirm the accepted exams for 2027-28.

What is the Integrated MBA?

It is a 4-year integrated management programme you can join after Class 12. It takes you from business fundamentals to MBA-level study in one programme.

Can I choose a dual specialisation?

Yes. A dual specialisation MBA lets you combine two fields, subject to availability. Your counsellor can explain the combinations offered.

Why study an MBA in Healthcare Management at SGT University?

SGT University has its own 1,000+ bed multispeciality hospital and a superspeciality Ayurveda hospital on campus, so Healthcare Management students learn in a working healthcare environment.

Is SGT University NAAC accredited?

Yes. SGT University is accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council).

Are scholarships available for MBA students?

Yes. Scholarships are offered under the university's scholarship policy for each academic year. Your counsellor can tell you which ones you may qualify for.

What placement support do MBA students get?

Students get dedicated placement support. Across all programmes, the university works with 275+ recruiters, including Deloitte, EY, Accenture, Amazon, Google, Infosys, TCS, Max Healthcare and Medanta.

Where is SGT University located?

The campus is on Gurgaon-Badli Road, Chandu, Budhera, Gurugram, Haryana 122505, in Delhi NCR.
